02Our story

Our story

UNLISTED YOUTH STREETWEAR started with a simple feeling: nobody hands you a map at this age. We make clothes for people who are still building who they are, and who are fine not having every answer yet.

2025

The question

Sketches, fabric swatches and a name that stuck. “Unlisted” wasn’t about hiding. It was about being off everyone’s list and open to whatever comes next.

2026

The first drop

The first run went out to friends, classmates and the first believers. Every piece carried the logo and a line we meant.

Now

The full range

Tees, armless tops, baggy jeans, jackets and socks. Loose fits, heavy fabric, one logo.

Next

More colours, more pieces and pop-ups on campuses across Ghana.

06FAQ

Questions

How do I order?

Add pieces to your bag, tap “Order on WhatsApp” and send the message. We confirm your size, colour and delivery with you there.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or cash on delivery. We’ll share the details on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed.

Do you deliver?

Yes, across Ghana. The delivery fee and time depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp.

What if my size doesn’t fit?

Message us on WhatsApp soon after delivery and we’ll sort out an exchange for unworn pieces.

Are the drops limited?

Yes. Each piece is made in a small run, and when it’s sold out it’s gone until the next drop.
